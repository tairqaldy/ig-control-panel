# Credits — terms

*Last updated: 17 August 2026. These terms cover Resurfly credits and form part of the [Terms of Service](/terms). Refunds are covered by the [Refund policy](/refunds).*

## What a credit is

A credit is a prepaid unit that pays for one metered action once your plan's monthly allowance for that action is used up.

- **1 credit = 1 save analyzed.**
- **1 credit = 1 Ask answer** (this includes a Profile score report, which runs the same kind of request).
- **1 credit = 20 automated replies.**

Nothing else costs credits. Importing, browsing, searching, exporting, building automation rules and using the simulator are free of credits on every plan, and credits never raise a per-minute rate limit.

## How they are spent

- Your plan allowance is always spent first. Credits are only touched when the allowance for that specific action has run out.
- Every grant, purchase and debit is written to a ledger you can read yourself in **Billing → Credits**, with the reason and the running balance, so the number can always be explained.
- For automated replies, where 1 credit covers 20 sends, we keep the unused part of a credit and spend it on the next sends rather than rounding up each time.
- If the balance is not enough for an action, the app says so and offers a top-up or an upgrade. It never charges you automatically for a pack you did not buy.

## Buying

- Packs are one-time purchases through Paddle, our merchant of record: **500 credits $12**, **2,000 credits $39**, **6,000 credits $99**. Prices are shown before checkout and include the VAT/sales tax Paddle calculates for your country.
- Buying a pack is not a subscription. It does not renew and it does not change your plan.
- A pack is credited to your account when Paddle confirms the payment, normally within seconds. The same transaction is never credited twice, even if Paddle sends the confirmation again.

## What credits are not

- Credits **have no cash value**. They are a prepaid entitlement to use the service, not money on deposit, and they are not an e-money product.
- Credits are **not transferable** between accounts and cannot be sold or gifted.
- Credits **do not expire** while your account exists.
- Cancelling a subscription does not remove your credits; they stay on the account and work again if you resubscribe. **Deleting your account forfeits any remaining balance**, so ask for a refund before you delete.

## Refunds

An **unused** pack can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase — write to hello@resurfly.com. If part of a pack is unused we refund that part at the pack's own per-credit price. **Spent credits are not refundable**, because the analysis, answers and replies they paid for have already been produced and cost us money at our providers. Full details in the [Refund policy](/refunds).

If we discontinue the service, unused credits are refunded pro rata.

## Changes

We may change what a credit covers or what a pack costs for **future** purchases, with notice in the product. Credits you have already bought keep the rate that applied when you bought them.
