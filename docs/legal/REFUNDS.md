# Refund policy — resurfly.com

*Last updated: 17 August 2026.*

- **First purchase: 14 days, no questions asked.** If you subscribe (Pro $19/month or $144/year, Studio $49/month or $348/year) and it isn't for you, e-mail hello@resurfly.com within 14 days of the first charge and we refund it in full. Paddle (our merchant of record) processes the refund to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.
- **Renewals:** if a renewal went through and you haven't used the service in that period, e-mail us within 7 days of the renewal and we refund it.
- **Cancelling** is always possible from Billing → Manage subscription; access continues to the end of the paid period and no further charges are made.
- **Credit packs** (500 credits $12, 2,000 credits $39, 6,000 credits $99) are prepaid. An **unused** pack can be refunded within 14 days of purchase — e-mail us and we refund it in full. **Credits that have been spent are not refundable**, because the analysis, answers and replies they paid for have already cost us money. If part of a pack is unused we refund that part at the pack's own per-credit price.
- **Credits do not expire** while your account exists, and they are not transferable to another account. Cancelling a subscription does not remove the credits; they stay on the account and work again the moment you resubscribe. Deleting your account forfeits any remaining balance, so ask for the refund before you delete.
- **Trials** are free — nothing to refund.
- Because analysis has real per-save costs on our side, we may decline repeat refunds on the same account after the first one, or where usage clearly exceeded a normal evaluation. We'll say so plainly if that's the case.
- **Export first:** before you cancel or ask for a refund, export your library (JSON, CSV, Markdown or Obsidian) from the Library page — exports work on every plan.

Questions: hello@resurfly.com
