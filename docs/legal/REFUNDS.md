# Refund policy — resurfly.com

*Last updated: 17 August 2026.*

- **First purchase: 14 days, no questions asked.** If you subscribe (monthly or yearly) and it isn't for you, e-mail hello@resurfly.com within 14 days of the first charge and we refund it in full. Paddle (our merchant of record) processes the refund to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.
- **Renewals:** if a renewal went through and you haven't used the service in that period, e-mail us within 7 days of the renewal and we refund it.
- **Cancelling** is always possible from Billing → Manage subscription; access continues to the end of the paid period and no further charges are made.
- **Trials** are free — nothing to refund.
- Because analysis has real per-save costs on our side, we may decline repeat refunds on the same account after the first one, or where usage clearly exceeded a normal evaluation. We'll say so plainly if that's the case.
- **Export first:** before you cancel or ask for a refund, export your library (JSON, CSV, Markdown or Obsidian) from the Library page — exports work on every plan.

Questions: hello@resurfly.com
