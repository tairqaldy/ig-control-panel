Resurfly Companion

Installed. Two steps left.

  1. Pin the extension: click the puzzle icon 🧩 in Chrome's toolbar → pin Resurfly Companion.
  2. In Resurfly open Import → Companion → Get pairing code, then paste the code below (or in the extension popup).

After that it runs on its own: every 6 hours it reads the posts you saved on Instagram (using your own logged-in browser) and sends them to your Resurfly library.

What this extension does

Every 6 hours (and when you press “Sync now”) it calls Instagram's own saved-posts endpoint /api/v1/feed/saved/posts/ in your browser, exactly like the instagram.com web app does, and sends the post metadata (link, caption, creator, thumbnail/video URLs, counts) to your Resurfly. It reads only what you saved. It never posts, likes, follows or messages.

You can also right-click any page on instagram.com and pick Sync my saves to Resurfly to run a sync straight away, without opening the popup.

The optional switch “Also harvest when my browser is closed” additionally sends three Instagram cookies (sessionid, csrftoken, ds_user_id) to your Resurfly, where they are stored encrypted and used only for the same read. Turning it off deletes them.
